ABOUT THE WORK

Softness is a form of response.

SQUISH is a browser-based interactive artwork. The mother jellyfish is shaped by 72 spring nodes, so every touch leaves a local impression instead of scaling the entire character.

A gentle touch creates expression and recoil. Pulling outward from any edge of the bell nurtures a baby with its own color, pattern, personality, emotions, and memories.
